\section{Example trajectories and planning times}
\label{app:visualisations}
Figure~\ref{fig:scenario-examples} spans four pages and shows stored
seed-0 full-solver traces, including a collision; these examples do not
replace B4's repeated-run statistics. Squares mark starts, stars goals,
and crosses unfinished drones' returned positions. Collision targets are
not logged. Each row identifies either the demo's 9.5\,s allowance or a
fixed-simulation experiment with a relaxed deadline. The wall-gap and
corridor layouts have seven and eight obstacles, beyond the demo's
five-obstacle range. The complete gallery covers all 76 evaluation
configurations and the supplied example, excluding development layouts.
