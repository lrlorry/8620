\subsubsection*{S3: component effects as complexity increases}
\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/component_effects.pdf}
\caption{Paired change in $L$ after removing a component at low/high factor
levels (open/filled markers); 95\% seed-block intervals.
Positive values favour the full solver.}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Guidance in expansion and rollout} has the largest measured effect.
Uniform proposals/rollouts raise $L$ by $\val{CompNoguidance}$
$\val{CompNoguidanceci}$ and change success by $\val{CompNoguidanceP}$\,pp.
The loss grows from $\val{CompNoguidanceClo}$ without obstacles to
$\val{CompNoguidanceChi}$ with five; the clutter interaction excludes zero
($\val{CompNoguidanceCint}$), whereas its state/action interactions do not.
This ablation removes both route guidance and admissible-target filtering.

\textbf{Root choice} also matters: most-visited selection raises $L$ by
$\val{CompVisitschoice}$ $\val{CompVisitschoiceci}$.
The increase is larger with clutter ($\val{CompVisitschoiceCint}$ interaction
interval) and smaller with richer commands ($\val{CompVisitschoiceAint}$).
These comparisons favour the chosen rule on these tasks.

\textbf{Widening, leaf estimates and risk surcharges} have no statistically
resolved effects across these corners (full estimates in
Appendix~\ref{app:components}). Routes are roughly eleven steps, comparable
to the sampled tree depth plus eight-step rollout, which may limit the
leaf estimate's contribution. The risk-free ablation retains route
guidance; its result therefore does not contradict the loss from
removing all guidance.

\textbf{Search versus its base policy.}
Removing search changes $L$ by $\val{CompHeuristic}$
$\val{CompHeuristicci}$, with no resolved interaction.
The evaluated completion performance is largely explained by
the guided base policy. In the additional regression/generated
comparison, multi-drone search rescues $\val{BaseMultiRescued}$ episodes and
loses $\val{BaseMultiLost}$; the pooled difference is inconclusive
(Appendix~\ref{app:regression}).
