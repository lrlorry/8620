The factorial covers two drones at $\alpha=0.25$, and S5 has only one layout

Fixed-simulation runs share the demo's trial cap, but omit its binding time

budget equivalence. Static

disconnection but uses static distances for remaining detours.
Successful wall-gap
runs average $\val{SuppGapSteps}$ steps for an $\val{SuppGapRoute}$-step
static route, suggesting an optimistic cutoff estimate; these experiments
do not isolate the cause (Figure~\ref{fig:risk-maps}, bottom right).
Finite-horizon value iteration on the supplied model gives maximum success
probabilities of
$\val{CeilGap}\%$ for the wall gap and $\val{CeilCorridor}\%$ for the corridor
(Appendix~\ref{app:supplement}). Both lie inside the solver's Wilson interval
from 13/20 successes. This demonstrates that some slip risk is unavoidable,
but does not establish optimality or attribute every observed failure to it.
Outcome progressive widening or an open-loop tree are possible follow-up
designs for improving successor reuse; neither was evaluated here.
